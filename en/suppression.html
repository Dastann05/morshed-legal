Morshed — Delete Your Account and Data

Android application app.morshed · Last updated: October 6, 2026 · Français

What to know first

Morshed keeps nothing about you with the developer, unless you open "Friends" to train with other people: that feature requires an account. Your sessions, your history and your settings still live in the app's private database, on your phone, and the day's session is composed there.

Outside the device, data can exist for four reasons, each one a choice you made: your "Friends" account if you signed in, the Android backup in your own Google account, the files you exported, and your email address on the testers list if the developer invited you to test the app. All of it can be deleted; the steps are below.

1. Delete the app's data (immediate)

  1. Open your phone's Settings.
  2. Go to Apps, then Morshed.
  3. Tap Storage, then Clear storage (Clear data on some phones).

Everything is deleted on the spot: sessions, imported history, templates, settings, chosen photos, account session and local backups. Deletion is permanent and the developer has no copy. Uninstalling the app has the same effect. On its own, it does not delete your "Friends" account on the server: see step 2.

2. Delete your "Friends" account (if you signed in)

Two ways, your choice:

  • In the app: Friends → My account → Delete my account.
  • From a browser, without the app: morshed-file.vercel.app/supprimer-mon-compte, then sign in to confirm.

Deletion is immediate: your account, your friends list, your invitations, your meetups, your challenges and your shared programs disappear from the server. A group session in progress erases itself 24 hours after it closes, whether or not you delete your account.

3. Delete the Android backup (if it is on)

If your phone's backup is on, Android may have copied the app's database and settings to your Google account. That copy belongs to you and the developer has no access to it. To delete it: Settings → Google → Backup, then delete the Morshed backup. You can also turn off backup for this app.

4. Remove yourself from the testers list (if you were invited)

If the developer invited you to test the app, your address is on the testers list. Since October 1, 2026, that list no longer decides who can sign in. To have your address removed, write to gagoun.prs@gmail.com from that address, with the subject "Morshed data deletion request". The same request also erases the lines of the server's technical log that carry your address, left over from versions before 0.39.0.

The request is handled within 30 days, in practice much faster, and you receive written confirmation.

Without writing to anyone, you can sign out from your profile page at any time, and remove access from your Google account or your Microsoft account.
