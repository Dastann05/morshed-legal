In brief

Morshed is a strength-training log. It helps you record your sessions and suggests new ones. It is not a coach and it is not a doctor. You stay in charge of what you lift and how you lift it. The app asks you to accept the terms below before you start, and again when they change.

Who publishes the app

Morshed is published by Dastann, an independent developer based in France. Contact: gagoun.prs@gmail.com.

What Morshed does

The app records your sessions, imports a history kept elsewhere, times your rests and suggests sessions. Those suggestions are computed on the phone by fixed rules written in advance, from what you entered: your latest loads, the muscles that got less work, your equipment and the time you have. No artificial intelligence model is involved. The app also offers capacity tests, which place your level on published standards.

These rules are general. They know nothing about your health, your medical history, your technique or the state of your equipment.

Your health comes before the session

  • Morshed gives no medical advice and is not a medical device. Nothing in the app replaces a doctor, a physiotherapist or a coach who watches you train.
  • Before starting a program, or returning after a long break, an injury, surgery, an illness or a pregnancy, ask a doctor. All the more if you have any doubt about your heart, your blood pressure or your joints.
  • Pain, feeling faint, dizziness, chest pain, unusual breathlessness: stop right away, and see a doctor.
  • A suggested load is a suggestion, not an instruction. You choose the load, the reps and when to stop. If it feels too heavy, it is.
  • Your technique, your equipment and your surroundings are your responsibility: safety bars set, fixings checked, floor clear, and a spotter when the exercise calls for one. The app's cues sum up a movement; they don't replace learning it.
  • Capacity tests go up to maximal effort. Take them only when you feel well and are warmed up, and never if a doctor has advised you against intense effort.

The "Friends" feature

No automated tool reads or filters this content: the developer reads every report and makes every decision himself.

You can delete your account at any time: the steps are on the deletion page.
